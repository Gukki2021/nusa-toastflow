1. How to Reserve a Meeting Slot

No login or password needed. Every slot is first come, first served and locks immediately upon submission.

1

Choose your meeting date

Open ToastFlow and select your target meeting month from the selector. You will see real-time availability for prepared speeches and meeting functionary roles.

💡 Our club meets on the second Friday of every month.
2

Pick your role or speech

Click Reserve a Speech to take a prepared speech slot (Speeches 1–4), or click Take role next to any open meeting functionary card (Timer, Ah-Counter, Evaluator, etc.).

💡 New members are encouraged to start with meeting functionary roles.
3

Enter details & project

Type your name (credentials match automatically from the club roster). For speeches, select your Pathway and Project — timings and objectives load automatically.

💡 You can add an optional speech title or note for your evaluator.
4

Submit & confirm

Click Reserve. Your booking immediately locks as Tentative to prevent others from double-booking. The VP Education reviews and confirms your slot for the official Program Sheet.

💡 To cancel or modify, contact the VP Education directly.

3. New to Toastmasters? Getting Started & Official Resources

Toastmasters International is a global non-profit educational organization dedicated to helping members build communication and leadership skills through a supportive, learn-by-doing meeting environment. Here is how your learning journey unfolds, paired directly with official resources.

What is Pathways?

Pathways is Toastmasters' modern learning experience designed around 5 core competencies: Public Speaking, Interpersonal Communication, Strategic Leadership, Management, and Confidence. Members choose from 11 specialized paths tailored to their personal and professional goals.

Step 1 · Meeting Foundation

Start with Functionary Roles: Timer & Ah-Counter

Why start here? Functionary roles carry minimal speaking pressure but train you to listen with acute attention. Serving as Timer teaches you meeting pacing and strict timekeeper signals (Green, Amber, Red). Serving as Ah-Counter trains your ear to catch hesitation sounds and filler words.

What to do: Pick the Timer or Ah-Counter slot for an upcoming meeting. Download the role script below and arrive 15 minutes before the meeting begins.

Step 2 · Impromptu Speaking

Participate in Table Topics

Why it matters: Every regular meeting features Table Topics, challenging members to give an impromptu 1- to 2-minute speech on an unannounced topic. It sharpens your ability to think on your feet, structure thoughts quickly (e.g. using PREP: Point, Reason, Example, Point), and speak with confidence without slides.

Prerequisite rule: Fulfilling a Table Topics speaker role is required before delivering your Level 1 Ice Breaker speech.

Step 3 · First Prepared Speech

Deliver Your Level 1 Ice Breaker

The milestone: Your Ice Breaker is a 4- to 6-minute speech introducing yourself to fellow club members. It is the very first project across all 11 Pathways tracks. Focus on sharing your story, milestones, or passions rather than aiming for technical perfection.

How to prepare: Log into Base Camp, activate your path, read the Ice Breaker project guide, and review the evaluation sheet with your assigned mentor.

Step 4 · Evaluation & Leadership

Provide Feedback as a Speech Evaluator

The core skill: Evaluation is the heart of the Toastmasters experience. As an Evaluator, you deliver a 2- to 3-minute verbal review using the CRC technique (Commend, Recommend, Commend). You practice attentive listening, positive reinforcement, and actionable advice.

Accessing evaluation forms: Every prepared speech has a standardized 3-page evaluation resource available in the official library and linked directly on our monthly Program Sheet.
