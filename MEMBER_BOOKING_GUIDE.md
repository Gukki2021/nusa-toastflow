# How to reserve a speech or meeting role

1. Open **ToastFlow**: https://gukki2021.github.io/nusa-toastflow/
2. Select the meeting month you want.
3. Choose **Reserve a Speech** or **Take role**.
4. Enter your name and select the meeting and role.
5. If you are giving a prepared speech, select your Pathway and project. You may also add a speech title or note.
6. Add your email or WhatsApp only if you would like the club to contact you. This is optional.
7. Select **Reserve** and wait for the message **“Reserved. See you at the meeting!”**
8. Refresh the page and check that your name still appears in the selected slot. If it does not, contact the VP Education—the reservation was not saved.

No account is required. Each slot is first come, first served. To change or cancel a confirmed reservation, contact the VP Education.

**Tentative** means a slot is reserved but still awaiting confirmation. It is not available for a second booking. Use **Register interest** or contact VP Education if you want to help with a tentative role. **Confirmed** means approved in the current programme.

## VP Education: export the programme

1. Open the online planner and sign in to **ExCo / Admin**.
2. Select the meeting date and check the saved holders, project text and confirmation states. The September import is for **11 September 2026**.
3. Choose **Generate Programming Sheet**. This reads the currently loaded database assignments.
4. Review every TBC, venue, speech duration and voting code. The right-hand column shows the reviewed name and credential; visiting club names belong on the left.
5. Use **Print / Save as PDF** or **Word**. Word export is an HTML-based `.doc`, not a native `.docx`.
6. **Share link** captures a snapshot, not a continuously updating sheet. After database changes, generate and share again. Inline **Edit text** changes are included in PDF/Word only and do not update the database.

The supplied September workbook has been reconciled once. Future workbook edits are not automatically imported into Supabase.

> The website must show **“Live shared availability is online.”** before you book. If it says reservations are unavailable, please contact the VP Education instead of submitting again.
